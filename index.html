6. Demonstration steps

  1. Apply level 0, then 1, then 2. The pump should remain ON.
  2. Apply level 3. The display should show 3, the pump should turn OFF, and FULL should turn ON.
  3. While level 3 remains selected, send three clock pulses. The alarm should turn ON at the third pulse.
  4. Press Manual reset. The counter returns to 00 and the alarm switches OFF.
